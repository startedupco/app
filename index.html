Frequently asked questions

When do university applications open for 2027 in South Africa?

Most universities open between March and May 2026. Closing dates vary by institution and programme.

Can I apply to multiple universities with one application?

Yes. One free profile covers universities, TVET colleges and bursaries.

How do I apply to a university in South Africa?

Create a free account, add your results and documents once, then apply from your dashboard.

How do I apply to a TVET college in South Africa?

Search colleges, check entry requirements, and apply online.

Is CollandUni free to use?

Signing up and browsing is free. Some institutions charge their own application fees.

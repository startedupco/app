What CollandUni does

CollandUni lets you apply to participating South African universities, TVET colleges and bursaries from one free profile. Create your account once, add your results and documents once, then apply to many institutions from your dashboard.

How it works

Create a free profile, check where you qualify with your APS, upload your ID and results, then submit applications for R60 each. Track every application from submitted to sent to accepted in one place.

Who we are

CollandUni is built by our small team under Helpdesk Central (Pty) Ltd, based in South Africa and focused on opening access to post-school opportunities.
